# Guild Assistant — First Beta (v0.1.0-beta.1)

Guild coordination and raid readiness for **any guild** on World of Warcraft: Forever. Open it with the minimap
button or `/ga`. Everything is done from the window. You only need to be in a guild.

## ✨ Features

### Any guild

- Works for any guild. No faction, realm or ruleset check.
- Each guild's data is kept separately on your computer. Your settings (minimap, language, windows) are shared by all
  your characters.
- Changing guild during a session asks for `/reload` to load the new guild's data.

### Name, commands and logo

- New name, folder and logo. Commands are `/ga` (or `/guildassistant`) and `/ga help`.

### Carried over

- **Profile and readiness:** your sheet, its source for each value, and a readiness checklist per raid.
- **Raid events:** events with rosters and assignments, attendance, templates, reports, and export and import.
- **Loot council:** the Loot card to answer after an encounter, and the voting window for officers and raid leaders.
- **PvP:** kill counter, kill ranking, recent kills, and PvP seasons with missions, points and rewards.
- **Guild tools:** professions lookup, blacklist alerts, layers with swap requests, and the guild map.
- **Sync:** changes made during an encounter are queued and sent when it ends.
