# Guild Assistant — First Release (v0.1.0)

Guild coordination, raid readiness and PvP for **any guild** on World of Warcraft: Forever. Open it with the minimap
button or `/ga`. This release brings roles from your guild ranks, announcements, groups, Intel and a reworked PvP.

## ✨ Features

### Roles from guild ranks

- Roles now come from the guild rank: guild master, co-guild master, officer, raid leader, roster, member and alt.
- New **Ranks** tab: the guild master maps each rank of the guild to a role. The guild master is always the guild
  master; a rank nobody mapped counts as member.
- Raid leaders manage the sign-ups, readiness rules, loot and members of their own roster.
- The sidebar is split into **Guild** and **Admin** groups, and PvP administration has its own tab.

### Modules

- New **Modules** tab: turn off the parts of the addon you don't use (PvP, Intel, groups, announcements, map, layers,
  blacklist and more). A **Reload the interface** button applies the change.

### Announcements

- The guild master and co-guild master publish, pin and delete announcements.
- Everyone sees new announcements in a popup, an unread badge in the sidebar and a banner on Home.

### Groups

- New **Groups** tab: post a group with the tank, healer and damage seats you need, apply to other groups, and accept
  or decline applicants.
- A notice tells you when someone in the guild opens a group, and Home shows the open groups.

### Intel and Kill on Sight

- Enemy players around you are detected and shared with the guild: alerts, enemy pins on the world map and minimap,
  and an Intel window with stats and reports.
- Personal Kill on Sight and ignore lists, plus a **guild Kill on Sight** list kept by the guild master and co-guild
  master. Mark enemies with the target button.
- World map buttons to show or hide guild and enemy pins. Commands: `/ga intel` and `/ga spy`.

### PvP

- Your PvP log no longer ends with the season: it keeps the last 45 days.
- New mission options, a "battlegrounds played" goal and bounty missions on Kill on Sight targets.
- Enemy classes and races follow your own faction, Alliance or Horde.
- The guild master and co-guild master see each member's mission progress.

### Installed check

- The guild master and co-guild master see who in the guild runs the addon and which version, and can ask online
  members for theirs.

### Professions and map

- Professions match across English and Spanish clients, and each crafter is listed once per recipe.
- Guild members show as diamond pins on the map.

### Quality of life

- The sidebar scrolls, and scrollbars can be dragged with the mouse.
- Text you are typing survives redraws and tab switches.
- The Loot card keeps its scroll position.
- Slash command words are case-insensitive.
